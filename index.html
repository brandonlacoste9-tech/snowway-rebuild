FAQ

Questions, answered.

What if my car is in the driveway?

We'll clear around it and make sure you aren't blocked in — but we can't remove snow from under or on top of the vehicle.

Can I book just for my vacation?

Yes — our minimum booking is one month. We recommend booking a bit before and after your trip so you never shovel the day you get home.

How does the neighbour discount work?

Sign up with a neighbour (within 1–3 houses or just across a quiet street) and you both get 10% off.

What does "unlimited visits" really mean?

Every snowfall of 1cm or more triggers a visit — no caps, no counting, all season long.
